🏛️ About 港大道 (HKU DAO)

港大道 (HKU DAO) is a decentralized autonomous organization built on the RBAS blockchain, dedicated to preserving and celebrating the rich heritage of the University of Hong Kong.

The University of Hong Kong (HKU), founded in 1911, is the oldest tertiary institution in Hong Kong. With over a century of excellence, HKU has shaped generations of leaders, thinkers, and innovators. 港大道 transforms HKU's legacy into a digital ecosystem where alumni, students, and enthusiasts can connect, trade, and co-create value through NFTs.

Our mission is to bridge the past and the future by tokenizing HKU's cultural assets – from historic buildings and academic milestones to student memories and iconic symbols. Each NFT represents a unique piece of HKU's story, secured on the RBAS blockchain.

🔑 Key Features

  • 🏛️ Faculty & Department NFTs – Own a piece of HKU's academic heritage.
  • 🌆 Iconic Buildings & Landmarks – Collect NFTs of HKU's most beloved structures.
  • 📜 History by Year – Trace HKU's journey through milestone NFTs.
  • 🍜 Local Culture – From dim sum to hiking trails, explore HKU's vibrant community. Tourist Spots.
  • 💎 NFT Marketplace – Buy, sell, and trade HKU NFTs with the community.
  • 🔗 RBAS Blockchain – Secure, transparent, and authentic digital ownership.
