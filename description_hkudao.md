# 港大道 (HKU DAO) – Project Overview

> **Preserving HKU's Heritage on the RBAS Blockchain**

---

## 📖 Overview

**港大道 (HKU DAO)** is a decentralized autonomous organization built on the **RBAS blockchain**, dedicated to preserving and celebrating the rich cultural heritage of the University of Hong Kong (HKU).

The platform transforms HKU's 110+ years of history into a vibrant digital ecosystem where alumni, students, and enthusiasts can explore, collect, and trade NFTs representing HKU's faculties, buildings, history, and local culture.

---

## 🏛️ The HKU Story

Founded in **1911**, the University of Hong Kong is the oldest tertiary institution in Hong Kong. For over a century, HKU has shaped generations of leaders, thinkers, and innovators across Asia and the world.

From its iconic Main Building to its world-class faculties, from the historic Eliot Hall to the bustling streets of the Centennial Campus – every corner of HKU tells a story.

**港大道** brings these stories to life on the blockchain.

---

## 🎯 Our Mission

> *"To bridge the past and the future by tokenizing HKU's cultural assets – from historic buildings and academic milestones to student memories and iconic symbols."*

We believe that heritage should be preserved, celebrated, and shared. By tokenizing HKU's cultural assets as NFTs, we create:

- **Digital ownership** – Own a piece of HKU's legacy
- **Authentic provenance** – Every NFT is secured on the RBAS blockchain
- **Community engagement** – Alumni, students, and supporters can connect and co-create
- **Educational value** – Learn about HKU's history through interactive NFTs

---

## 🏗️ The Three-Level NFT Hierarchy

The application follows a **three-level+ hierarchical structure**, making it easy to navigate HKU's rich ecosystem:

```
Level 1: HKU DAO (Main page)
    └── Level 2: Categories (e.g., Faculties, Buildings, History)
        └── Level 3: Subcategories (e.g., Departments, Building Rooms)
            └── Level 3.5: Items* (e.g., Courses, Lecture Halls)
```

*Currently, Items and Subcategories are combined into one unified level.*

### Level 1 – Categories (🏛️)

Categories represent the major pillars of HKU's identity:

- **Faculties** – Architecture, Arts, Business, Dentistry, Education, Engineering, Law, Medicine, Science, Social Sciences
- **Main Campus Buildings** – Main Building, Haking Wong, Chow Yei Ching, K.K. Leung, Meng Wah Complex, and more
- **Centennial Campus** – Cheng Yu Tung Tower, Chi Wah Learning Commons, Lee Shau Kee Lecture Centre
- **Halls & Residences** – Eliot Hall, May Hall, Swire Hall, Jockey Club Student Villages
- **Medical Campus** – Faculty of Medicine Building, Pauline Chan Building, Patrick Manson Building
- **Sports Facilities** – Stanley Ho Sports Centre, Henry Fok Health and Fitness Complex
- **History** – Decade-by-decade milestones (1910–2029)
- **Local Culture** – Dim sum, hiking trails, two-dish rice, siu mei

### Level 2 – Subcategories (📚)

Each category contains subcategories that dive deeper:

- **Faculty Subcategories** – Departments and schools (e.g., Department of Architecture, School of Chinese)
- **Building Subcategories** – Rooms, floors, and lecture halls
- **Hall Subcategories** – Individual floors and sections

### Level 3 – Items (🎓)

Items represent the most granular level of the hierarchy:

- **Courses** – Individual classes and programs
- **Lecture Halls** – Specific rooms and theaters
- **Professors** – Distinguished faculty members
- **Student Memories** – Iconic student experiences

## 🛡️ Technology Stack

| Layer | Technology |
|-------|------------|
| **Frontend** | HTML5, CSS3, Vanilla JS (no framework) |
| **Backend** | Node.js + Express.js |
| **Blockchain** | RBAS (WebSocket connection) |
| **Storage** | JSON files (no database) |
| **Deployment** | Any Node.js host (PM2 recommended) |

### Why RBAS Blockchain?

RBAS provides:

- **Decentralized ownership** – NFTs are truly owned by users
- **Transparent transactions** – All transfers are recorded on-chain
- **Secure authentication** – Wallet-based login and verification
- **Low cost** – RBAS is designed for scalable NFT applications
